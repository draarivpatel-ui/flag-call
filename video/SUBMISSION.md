# Flag Call | Girls Who Code HHS Hackathon 2026

Name: Flag Call, a heat safety decision game

Description:
Flag Call puts you in the coach's shoes on a hot August practice field. Live sliders for air temperature, humidity, radiant heat, and wind feed a wet bulb globe temperature readout with the arithmetic of the Stull approximation inside, and a five tier flag scale tells you where conditions stand. Eight city presets capture real conditions from Houston at 101 F to Phoenix at 118 F. Choose practice schedules block by block and make the call, full practice, helmets only, no equipment, or postpone, with one tap judge shortcuts to see an unsafe plan or the safe call. A nine step Day 1 walkthrough teaches the whole system, and the Day Report grades your calls. Playtesting was conducted entirely by automated bots over exactly 592,704 simulated schedule combinations, each checked against an independent oracle for tier bands, determinism, and scoring consistency. Twenty unit test files plus 158 engine content assertions and 34 accessibility checkpoints all pass.
