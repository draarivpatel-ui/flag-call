This is Flag Call, a heat safety decision game for student athletics. You are the coach or athletic trainer on the field, and the call is yours. Four live weather sliders, air temperature, humidity, radiant heat, and wind, feed a wet bulb globe temperature readout computed from the same science real guidelines use. The tier chip tells you where you stand. Normal, watch, caution, warning, or danger. Eight city presets capture real August conditions, from Houston at one hundred one degrees to Phoenix at one hundred eighteen. Pick a practice schedule and make the flag call for each block. Full practice, helmets only, no equipment, or postpone. The judge shortcuts show you an unsafe plan or the safe call instantly, and the Day Report grades your decisions. A nine step walkthrough teaches the whole system on day one. Every playtest in this game was run by automated bots over five hundred ninety two thousand simulated schedules, so the safety logic has been stress tested far beyond any afternoon of human play.
