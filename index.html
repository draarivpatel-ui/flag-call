Flag Call

The wet bulb globe temperature decision game for hot practice days

Read the heat. Make the call.

One flag decides whether practice runs, shortens, or shuts down. Blend air temperature, humidity, radiant heat, and wind into one wet bulb globe temperature reading, then call the right flag for every block of a real activity schedule.

Dry bulb air temperature in degrees Fahrenheit.

Moisture in the air. High humidity stalls sweat evaporation.

Sunlight load on a black globe, from overcast to full sun.

Moving air cools the globe and helps sweat do its job.
